# Release Notes: MultiAppShare v1.9.4

### Groups and history

- Stable group IDs so home shortcuts survive rename.
- Re-share a specific History row when a payload was saved.
- Encrypted backup can include theme, language, share delay, and last payload.

### Sharing

- Filter groups even when you only have a few.
- Failed handoff stays on that app so you can Retry.
- Share text or a file from the clipboard via the menu.
