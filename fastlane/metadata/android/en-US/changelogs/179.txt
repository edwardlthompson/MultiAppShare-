# Release Notes: MultiAppShare v1.9.3

### Share session

- Resume an in-flight share after the app process is killed (2-hour snapshot).
- Re-share the last payload from History.
- Skip this app or skip remaining; notification Next / Skip / Cancel.
- Optional delay between steps; persistable URI grants when the host allows them.

### Groups and settings

- Rename, duplicate, and merge groups; undo delete.
- Tablet two-pane editor; in-app language and theme (system / light / dark).
